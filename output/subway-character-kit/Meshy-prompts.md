# Subway character kit — exactly 20 characters

Created from your 20 supplied concepts, with individual fictional character references generated using the built-in imagegen tool. Batch 1: 01–10. Batch 2: 11–20.

## How to use

Upload the corresponding PNG to Meshy's Image to 3D flow. If a text field is available, paste the complete prompt below. In Text to 3D, paste the complete prompt directly. Generate one person per model. Request a neutral T-pose and preserve separated limbs; do not ask for sleeping, flips or dancing in the base mesh. Export FBX for the Mixamo upload workflow. Keep props separate. These reference images are not rigged 3D assets; geometry, hands, textures and deformation need inspection after generation. Character behavior belongs to later animations and game logic, not the model-generation prompt.

The two batches organize the deliverables; they do not guarantee the service will generate or export ten usable models per batch within its credit allowance. Account use must follow the service's terms.
